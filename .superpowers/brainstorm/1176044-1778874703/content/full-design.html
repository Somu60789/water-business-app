My top additions beyond the PDF scope:
1. Phone OTP login (not email — Indian market) via Firebase Auth
2. Subscription orders (recurring weekly/monthly) — core revenue driver
3. OTP delivery confirmation — prevents fraud
4. 1-tap reorder from history — reduces friction
5. Delivery time slots — morning/afternoon/evening slot picker
