One more question — Payment Gateway

Which payment method should customers use to pay for orders?

A

Razorpay (Recommended)

Best for India. Supports UPI, cards, net banking, wallets. Easy Flutter SDK integration.

  • 2% transaction fee
  • UPI / PhonePe / GPay / Paytm all work
  • Instant settlement
  • Simple dashboard to track payments
B

Cash on Delivery Only

No payment gateway needed. Delivery person collects cash. Simpler to start, no transaction fees.

  • No setup required
  • Zero transaction fees
  • Common for water delivery businesses
  • Online payment can be added later
C

Both — COD + Razorpay

Customer chooses at checkout. Most flexible. Slightly more work to build but covers all customers.

  • Maximum customer coverage
  • COD for trust-builders, online for convenience
  • Most water delivery apps offer both
